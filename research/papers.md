# Paper register

The selection window is 23 November 2023 to 23 November 2026. Dates below refer to the version identified, with earlier working versions noted where relevant. The input notes mixed studies, software documentation, interviews and reported profits. Each entry says which part, if any, became executable code.

## Two hypotheses implemented

| Research | Method in the source | Adaptation in this lab | What the result can answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zarattini, Barbon and Aziz, *A Profitable Day Trading Strategy for the U.S. Equity Market* (2024). [Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284) · [University copy](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/entities/publication/ab50f49b-4d5c-4aa8-aaa1-af7a4c131008) · [Coded recreation](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/) | Screens US stocks in play by opening relative volume. The first five minutes set the range and direction. Cross-sectional selection, risk sizing and intraday exits are material to the published strategy. | Use one ETF. Require a green opening five-minute candle, first-five volume at or above its prior 14-session mean, and the 09:59 close above the first-five high. One delayed long entry and a scheduled exit. See the breakout vote in [experiment.py](../intraday/experiment.py). | Whether that narrow price-and-volume rule adds value on SPY at a fixed clock. Stock selection, short trades and published returns have not been replicated. |
| Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon, *Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday Momentum Strategy for S&P500 ETF (SPY)* (2024). [Author page](https://concretumgroup.com/beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-sp500-etf-spy/) · [Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4824172) | Estimates ordinary movement at each time of day using prior sessions. A move outside a noise area can trigger a directional trade. The source also handles opening gaps, repeated entry opportunities, VWAP exits and exposure sizing. | Estimate the mean absolute open-to-10:00 return from the previous 14 valid sessions. Require the 09:59 close to exceed both the open-adjusted upper band and the opening half-hour VWAP proxy. One vote per session. [Band implementation](../intraday/experiment.py) | Whether an unusually strong first half-hour confirms a breakout. Fixed share count, bar-based VWAP and a fixed exit materially change the paper’s strategy. |

Both studies have the same three authors and both rules observe the same opening prices. An affirmative second vote can simply repeat the information in the first. The relevant comparison is the agreement strategy against each component, with exposure, costs and flat days visible. The common baseline buys the same share at the same time whenever an opening is eligible.

## Evidence that changed the engineering

| Source | Finding or scope | Implementation or decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Implementation Risk in Portfolio Backtesting, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20319) | Engines can disagree when they implement the same intended strategy differently. | An external event engine must reproduce every simulated fill, fee and final equity. A separate vector calculation reconstructs the features and votes. |
| [Look-Ahead-Bench, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13770) | A language model can draw on knowledge of the period it is supposedly predicting. | Reviewer inputs omit date, ticker, returns after the decision and fills. Sealing reduces obvious clues. Historical model contamination can still exist. |
| [TradingAgents, 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20138) and [Trading-R1, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11420) | The first decomposes a trading decision across roles. The second studies learned financial reasoning. | Two optional local LLM reviewers receive the same blind packet and can disagree. Their prose never determines the ledger. The tested AI is a logistic regression trained on resolved hypothetical trades. |
| [StockBench, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02209) and [Rohan Ray, Dartmouth undergraduate thesis, 2026](https://digitalcommons.dartmouth.edu/cs_senior_theses/68/) | StockBench evaluates sequential decisions. Ray compared external signals, models and seeds across 150 runs. Some signals changed behaviour irrespective of predictive usefulness. | Preserve individual decisions and counterfactual outcomes. Directly compare the same entry rule with and without the ML veto. No claim that an impressive explanation improves profit. |
| [Agentic Trading: When LLM Agents Meet Financial Markets, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19337) | Survey of agent design and evaluation, including the difficulty of comparing reported results. | Record data, clocks, threshold and costs in a versioned protocol. A survey supplies context, not an entry rule. |
| [AlphaForgeBench, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18481) and [BacktestBench, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17937) | Evaluate AI-generated executable strategies or implementations against controlled tasks. | Fixed ablations and executable checks make suggested strategies falsifiable. Published benchmark scores are not trading results from this project. |
| [Vibe-Trading, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24845) | Agent-native research workflow and traceability. | Keep a protocol, source manifest, result tables and review packet. The dated trial record distinguishes a retrospective simulation from a prospective observation. |
| [Kronos, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02739) and [FinRL-X, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21330) | Financial sequence forecasting and modular AI-trading infrastructure respectively. | Deferred model variants. A future trial needs to document when weights were trained, how outputs become orders and whether they improve the simple baseline on untouched data. |
